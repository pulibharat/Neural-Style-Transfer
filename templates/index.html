Inspiration & research

This demo builds on neural style transfer and AdaIN. These papers and images illustrate the ideas behind the project.

Key papers

  • A Neural Algorithm of Artistic Style Gatys, Ecker & Bethge — 2015 · arXiv:1508.06576
  • Perceptual Losses for Real-Time Style Transfer and Super-Resolution Johnson, Alahi & Fei-Fei — 2016 · arXiv:1603.03417
  • Arbitrary Style Transfer in Real-time with Adaptive Instance Normalization Huang & Belongie — 2017 (AdaIN, used in this app) · arXiv:1703.08092
  • Universal Style Transfer via Feature Transforms Li et al. — 2018 · arXiv:1807.10201
